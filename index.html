01 · About

Making medical images speak clearly

I'm an AI researcher with an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence & Robotics from the University of Tabriz, where my thesis on hybrid deep learning for brain tumor segmentation earned a perfect 20/20 under the supervision of Prof. Jafar Tanha.

My work sits at the intersection of computer vision and clinical need: weakly- and semi-supervised segmentation that learns from scarce annotations, Mamba–CNN hybrid architectures, topology- and geometry-aware knowledge distillation, and self-supervised synthesis of MRI from CT to bridge the modality gap.

I've published in Computers in Biology and Medicine, review for the International Journal of Imaging Systems and Technology, and I'm currently seeking PhD opportunities and research collaborations in medical image analysis.

02 · Research focus

Three threads, one goal

Less annotation, more anatomy, sharper images — each line of work removes a different bottleneck between raw scans and clinical insight.

THREAD / 01

Label-efficient segmentation

Weakly- and semi-supervised training with consistency guidance and pseudo-labeling, so models learn tumor boundaries from a fraction of the usual annotations.

THREAD / 02

Cross-modality synthesis

Self-supervised generation of MRI from CT using hierarchical vision priors and state-space models — soft-tissue contrast without a second scan.

THREAD / 03

Hybrid architectures

Mamba–CNN networks, receptive-field-enhanced skip connections, and topology-aware distillation that compress strong models without losing anatomical structure.
