About

Making medical images speak clearly

I'm an AI researcher with an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence & Robotics from the University of Tabriz, where my thesis on hybrid deep learning for brain tumor segmentation in MRI earned a perfect 20/20 under the supervision of Prof. Jafar Tanha.

My work sits at the intersection of computer vision and clinical need: weakly- and semi-supervised segmentation that learns from scarce annotations, Mamba–CNN hybrid architectures, knowledge distillation that respects anatomy's topology and geometry, and self-supervised synthesis of MRI from CT to bridge the modality gap.

I've published in Computers in Biology and Medicine and serve as a reviewer for the International Journal of Imaging Systems and Technology. I'm currently seeking PhD opportunities and research collaborations in medical image analysis.
